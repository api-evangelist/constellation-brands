Inspired by this philosophy, the Robert Mondavi Winery and James Beard Award-winning cookbook author and chef Andy Baraghani partnered to reimagine ‘family meal’ -- the cherished restaurant tradition that brings teams around the table to connect, unwind, and share in something meaningful.
