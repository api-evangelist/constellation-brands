Six brands in Constellation’s high-end imported beer portfolio were recognized by Impact Databank and M. Shanken Communications as “Hot Brands” in 2025: Pacifico Victoria Corona Non-Alcoholic Corona Familiar Corona Sunbrew Modelo Ranch Water Impact Databank evaluates brands based on meaningful growth criteria, including double-digit gains over multiple years, significant year-over-year growth, category leadership, and successful innovation launches. Pacifico Maintains Strong Momentum The Pacifico brand remained a standout among imported beer Hot Brands, growing 14.3% in 2025 to nearly 28.8 mil
