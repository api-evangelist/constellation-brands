Constellation Brands’ 2025 Early Career Professionals (ECP) Case Competition and annual flagship program: Brought together early-career professionals from across the organization to solve real business challenges and cultivate future leaders. Engaged more than 60 early-career employees, 15 employee coaches, and more than 20 employee judges. Focused on developing strategies that empower employees to authentically champion Constellation and its products.
