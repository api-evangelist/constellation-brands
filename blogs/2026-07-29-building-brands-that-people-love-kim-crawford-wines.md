Since launching in 1996 in Auckland, New Zealand, the Kim Crawford brand has built its reputation by taking a unique and unparalleled approach to winemaking. As one of New Zealand's pioneering wine brands, Kim Crawford helped introduce consumers around the world to the vibrant, expressive character of Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc while remaining approachable and unpretentious. Today, its mission is simple: capture the spirit of New Zealand with authentic, high-quality wines that spark joyful connection.
