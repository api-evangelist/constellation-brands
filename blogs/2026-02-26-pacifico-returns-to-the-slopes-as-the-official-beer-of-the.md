Pacifico : The Official Beer of the Snow League The world’s top snowboarders are back in action this weekend as the Snow League returns to Aspen – and Pacifico, the No. 11 beer brand by dollar sales in 2025 1 – is back as the event’s official beer. The brand will once again be fully integrated into the two-day event – headlined by its Pacifico Lodge, where fans can: Catch live competition feeds and enjoy the ultimate après‑ski atmosphere with crisp Pacifico beer.
