Modelo is "La Cerveza del Fútbol" For 30 years, Modelo has been embedded in fútbol culture – and the brand has more recently cemented its place in the sport as “La Cerveza del Fútbol.” In 2026, the brand is stepping up its presence around the game to help fans everywhere celebrate their passion for the sport. And as fútbol’s popularity surges across the U.S., the brand is uniquely positioned to lead the moment. In fact: More than 70% of beer drinkers associate Modelo with fútbol—more than any competitor in the beer category.
