In April 2026, Constellation Brands entered a new chapter with Nick Fink succeeding Bill Newlands as the fifth President and CEO in the company’s 80-year history. Since his first day on April 13, Nick has focused on meeting employees, engaging distributors and retailers, connecting with investors, and embedding himself into the company’s long-term growth strategy centered on consumer obsession, iconic brands, and operational excellence. Within two weeks of becoming President and CEO, Nick addressed thousands of employees about his priorities during a company town hall.
