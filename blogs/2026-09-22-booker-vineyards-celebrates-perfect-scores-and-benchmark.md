Booker Vineyard has reached a remarkable milestone, earning its highest accolades ever in leading wine critic Jeb Dunnuck ’s annual Paso Robles Report . The recognition includes perfect 100-point scores for two Booker wines, the 2024 Fracture and 2023 ONES Syrah. “These latest Booker releases are flat-out sensational, and I think clearly the finest lineup as a whole I've tasted from them, including two perfect wines,” said Dunnuck .
